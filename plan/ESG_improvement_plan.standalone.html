1 · Summary

The business is growing. The upside is in mix and repeat visits, not capacity.

Every 2026 month is 13–31% above the same month of 2025 and September is the best month per day you have ever had. At the same time the four boxes are only about half busy even at the 14:00–17:00 peak, 88% of customers buy the cheapest product, and 60% of cars never come back. Fixing those three things is worth roughly +12,000–15,000 ₾ a month within a quarter, with no new equipment.

What is working

  • Volume and ticket are both up — growth is real, not just price.
  • Managers price sensibly: 120 over-list charges (+1,697 ₾) vs 66 under-list (−782 ₾).
  • Weekends are strong: Saturday 1,860 ₾/day, Sunday 1,770 ₾/day.
  • A loyal core exists: 559 plates came back 2+ times, 51 came 5+ times.
  • The Reno fleet contract works — a proven B2B model to copy.
  • Labour is variable (35–40% per wash), so slow days do not sink the month.
  • Large headroom: 4.7 cars per peak hour across 4 boxes.

What is holding it back

  • No middle product. Standard 30–50 ₾, then VIP at 100–170 ₾ (3× jump). Nothing in between sells.
  • No retention. 2,741 plates, 60% one-time; phone captured on 1% of visits.
  • Empty mornings and weak Tue/Wed. 13% of washes before 12:00; Tue/Wed ≈ 1,220 ₾/day vs 1,650–1,860 Thu–Sun.
  • Leaks ≈ 1,500 ₾/month: comp washes booked as revenue, unpaid tabs, price typos (one 501 ₾ entry).
  • Data discipline: 13 days logged entirely to Box 1; 4 shifts never closed; May 2026 has no records.
  • Cash control: 71% cash, no reconciliation at shift close; card share varies 17–35% by manager.

Three priorities

1. Sell more per car — add-ons and a “სტანდარტი+” tier fill the 40→120 ₾ gap. 2. Bring cars back — plate-based loyalty and a mall-staff pass. 3. Fill dead hours — morning wax, Tue/Wed double stamps, fleet contracts. Underneath all three: fix the leaks and the shift-close controls.

4 · Leaks, controls and team

Money that is lost, phantom, or unverified

Leaks — ≈ 1,500 ₾ / month

  • Comp washes booked as revenue. 79 “talon” washes, 2,958 ₾ (≈ 850 ₾/month) with no cash behind them — the same names repeatedly (ES-001-GI ×14, MISHA ×7, VACHE ×6, WW-948-UW ×8, KOPE ×3 incl. two VIPs). Washers were paid ≈ 1,000 ₾ on them and they count toward manager bonus thresholds.
  • Unpaid tabs closed as pending: 11 washes, 532 ₾ never collected.
  • Price typos move money: one 50 ₾ wash entered as 501 ₾ (revenue overstated 451 ₾, that box's washer overpaid ≈ 160 ₾); nine washes entered as 2–5 ₾ instead of 25–50 ₾.

Data discipline that breaks the pay system

  • On 13 days every car was logged to Box 1 — including 58-, 48- and 46-car days: 502 cars, 24,000 ₾ (15% of revenue) attributed to one box's washers.
  • On ≈ 8 days the whole morning was typed in one afternoon batch (25 cars in 17 minutes on 23 Jul); the live dashboard was blank for those hours.
  • 4 shifts were never closed, so two days' cars merged into one archive (fixed in the app this week).
  • Bonus is a cliff (1,590 ₾ = nothing, 1,600 ₾ = 50 ₾) on a total that includes comp, Reno and unpaid washes; the 10% VIP bonus is paid on unpaid VIPs.

Cash control

71% of revenue is cash. Nothing at shift close reconciles the card terminal or the counted cash against the system. Card share by manager: ACHIKO 35% · DATO 29% · SABA 28% · ILIA 17% — one of ILIA's 23-car days shows zero card payments. This may simply be habit; the point is that today it is unverifiable. A one-minute reconciliation step at close (terminal total + counted cash, difference stored in the archive) closes the gap.

5 · The plan — six levers

Sell more per car and bring cars back

1Add-ons: sell VIP's ingredients one at a time

+2,500–4,000 ₾ / mo

Standard already includes vacuum and shine; the things that make VIP “VIP” (wax, tyre silicone, fragrance) are only sold as a 100–170 ₾ bundle. Offer them on top of any wash: wax 15/20 ₾, tyre silicone 5, fragrance 5, leather care 20, plastic dressing 20 — plus the existing underbody 15 and pet-hair 20. Material cost is 0.5–2 ₾ each.

  • Rule for managers: offer one add-on to every car, every time (“ცვილს დავამატებთ? +20 ლარი, სამ თვეს იცავს”).
  • Washers get the same 35% on add-ons so they push them too; you still keep ≈ 60% margin.
  • 15% attach rate ≈ 5 cars/day × 17 ₾ ≈ 2,500 ₾/month; a good counter reaches 25%+.

2“სტანდარტი+” tier and a proper VIP ladder

+2,000 ₾ / mo (≈ 1,000 margin)

Name one bundle so the ladder reads 40 → 55 → 120: სტანდარტი+ = standard + wax + tyre silicone at 45 / 55 / 65 / 75 ₾ (sedan / jeep / XL / minivan). Put XL VIP on the menu at 150 / 170 — customers already pay it, it just has no fixed price. Add მინივენი as a car type.

  • Show the ladder at the counter so 120 ₾ has a reference point instead of looking like 3× the price.
  • Track VIP share per manager on the live screen; target 9% team-wide (from 5.6%). Pay the 10% VIP bonus only on paid VIPs.

3Plate-based loyalty — no app, no phone needed

+3,000 ₾ / mo

The ERP already recognises every plate. Every 6th wash free: the manager sees “5/6” when typing the plate and tells the customer. It is a 16.7% discount that only ever reaches people who came six times — it pays for itself by pulling forward visits 2, 3 and 4.

  • Ask for the phone at the 3rd visit (“we'll text you when your free wash is close”); SMS reminder every 3 weeks (≈ 0.05 ₾ each).
  • Monthly pass for mall staff: 4 standard washes for 100 / 130 / 160 ₾, prepaid. 50 passes ≈ 5,000–6,500 ₾ prepaid, about half of it incremental.
  • If repeat washes move from 41% to 50% of volume, that alone is ≈ 3,000 ₾/month.

5 · The plan — six levers (continued)

Fill dead hours and keep the money you earn

4Sell the detailing service in the dead morning hours

+2,000–2,800 ₾ / mo

A 300 ₾+ product that sells ≈ 0 times a month, and boxes that are empty from 10:00 to 12:30. One detailing slot per day, mornings only, bookable through the existing QR app with a 50 ₾ deposit. Before/after photos on Instagram and TikTok, a laminated album at the counter, 20 ₾ flat to the manager per detail sold. Six to eight details a month uses capacity that earns nothing today.

5Fill Tuesday/Wednesday and mornings — without cutting peak prices

+3,000–4,400 ₾ / mo

Utilisation is ≈ 50% even at peak, so discounts at peak are unnecessary; off-peak incentives are almost free money.

  • Morning gift, weekdays before 12:30: wax free (costs 1–2 ₾ in material and trains customers to value wax).
  • Loyalty stamps count double on Tuesday and Wednesday.
  • Fleet contracts on the Reno model, off-peak only: mall tenants' cars, Bolt/taxi drivers, courier vans, nearby dealerships (pre-delivery washes), rental companies — 15–20% off with a monthly invoice, valid Mon–Wed or before 13:00. Three fleets × 40 washes ≈ 3,000 ₾/month.
  • Mall partnership: “drop the car, shop, pick it up” — a 10% code on mall receipts, signage at the parking entrance, inclusion in tenant promotions.
  • Deliver the evening or change the poster: 20:00–21:00 produced 35 washes in 107 days, yet the late September nights coincided with the best month per day.

6Keep the money you already earn

+1,500 ₾ / mo + control
  • A separate comp category (staff and owner cars) that does not count as revenue or toward bonus thresholds; washer pay on comps becomes a deliberate choice.
  • No shift close with unpaid tabs unless a name and phone are recorded.
  • Price sanity check at entry (warn outside 50–200% of the usual price), mandatory box selection, and a card-terminal / cash-count reconciliation step at close that stores the difference in the archive.
  • Bonus on paid revenue only; consider a percentage above 1,600 ₾ instead of a cliff.
  • Enter fixed costs monthly so the net line in the master sheet is real.

What not to do

Do not cut standard prices — they are market-normal and customers already accept 35/55/60 over-list charges without complaint. Do not add a fifth box — the four you have are half empty at peak. Do not launch a customer app — the plate already does the job.
